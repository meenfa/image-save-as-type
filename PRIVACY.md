# Privacy Policy — SavePix: Save Images in Your Preferred Format

**Last updated: November 22, 2026**

SavePix helps you download a webpage image in its original format or convert it to PNG, JPG, or WebP. This policy explains what information the extension uses when you choose one of its image menu options.

## Information the extension processes

When you choose an image format from the extension's right-click menu, the extension reads the URL of the image you selected and retrieves that image so it can download or convert it. The request goes to the website hosting the image and may use your browser's existing login credentials for that website, where applicable, so images that require your session can be retrieved.

The selected image and its URL are processed locally in your browser to create the download. The extension does not send them to the extension developer or to an analytics or advertising service. The extension does not collect browsing history, account details, or other page content for its own use.

## Storage and sharing

The extension does not save the selected image or its URL in a developer-controlled database or transmit them to the developer. Image data is held temporarily in browser memory while the selected download is prepared, then released. The image request itself is sent to the website hosting that image, subject to that website's own privacy practices.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe those changes.
